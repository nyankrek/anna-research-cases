CareerBoost AI:
from CV to feedback.

What if feedback on a CV were easier to understand — and easier to act on?

SCROLL TO EXPLORE ↓

THE CONCEPT

A guided path from CV upload to a better draft.

In a Figma applied prototyping course, our group explored a service for early-career job seekers who struggle to structure and tailor a CV. The concept used AI-style feedback to suggest changes while leaving the decision to accept them with the user.

CONTEXTFigma course project
MY ROLEIdeation, user flow, prototyping & testing
OUTPUTConnected mobile concept
01 / FRAME

Make the broad problem small enough to design.

Our group reviewed competing services and collected useful patterns. I contributed ideas about the problem and a possible user, then mapped the main journey: upload a CV, confirm the target role and country, review suggestions, choose what to apply and save the result. The flow focused the prototype on eight screens.

02 / MAKE

Move from sketches to a connected mobile flow.

I sketched screen ideas, made low-fidelity wireframes and moved the concept from an initial desktop direction to a mobile app. The high-fidelity prototype joined the target-job check, section-level suggestions and saved CV versions.

03 / LEARN

Make the next action obvious.

I asked a small number of students to work through the scenario and noted where they hesitated. Their feedback led me to make approval controls more visible and add an option to apply all suggested changes.

OBSERVATION → ITERATION

Suggestions help only when people can clearly choose what to accept.

04 / OUTCOME

A testable concept, not a shipped product.

The result was a connected prototype and a clearer path from AI-style feedback to user decisions. This was a classroom usability check, not a measured product launch.
